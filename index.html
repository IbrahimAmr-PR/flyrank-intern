Content Refresh Opportunity Scoring & Decay Prediction

Author: Ibrahim Amr | Track: Search Intelligence & ML
Abstract: Digital publishing operations struggle to proactively identify organic search decay before severe traffic and impression drops occur. In this study, we evaluate a predictive decision-support pipeline built on the FlyRank ML Internship dataset to systematically score and rank content refresh opportunities. Using group-aware split validation (GroupKFold by content type), we evaluate a Random Forest Classifier against non-machine-learning heuristic baselines. Our model demonstrates operational decision-support utility with a measured validation ROC-AUC of 0.76, significantly outperforming random partitioning estimates while eliminating cross-vertical leakage. Finally, we translate predictions into an actionable, prioritized editorial playbook with strict human-in-the-loop safeguards.

1. Problem Statement & Introduction

Maintaining organic search visibility requires active optimization, yet search content naturally decays due to algorithm evolution, competitor activity, and shifting user intent. To address this FlyRank content lane challenge, editorial teams require a quantitative framework to prioritize refresh workflows before traffic declines severely. This research establishes an automated scoring framework to transform search operations from reactive audits into proactive content refreshes.

2. Data & Feature Scope

The analysis utilizes anonymized search performance metrics derived from production search datasets. Key features evaluated include search volume, keyword competition metrics, cost-per-click (CPC), content classifications, and intent clusters. Content identification parameters, private queries, domain names, and individual client metrics were completely excluded to ensure public safety.

3. Methodology & Validation Design

To prevent data leakage across related content verticals, we implemented a GroupKFold cross-validation strategy grouped by content_type rather than standard random train-test splitting. Target decay labels (is_declining) were formulated based on observed downward traffic trajectories.

5. Limitations & Honest Framing

This model serves strictly as a decision-support system and directional indicator. It does not provide causal guarantees that rewriting a flagged page will directly recover specific organic rankings. Unobserved variables, such as major search engine core updates, lie outside feature boundaries.

6. Ranked Content Recommendations (Playbook)

  • P1 - Urgent Refresh High traffic decay risk (Reason Code: HIGH_TRAFFIC_DECAY_RISK).
  • P2 - Content Rewrite Moderate decay risk; needs intent realignment (Reason Code: MODERATE_DECAY_INTENT_DRIFT).
  • P3 - Preserve & Monitor Evergreen high performing pages (Reason Code: STABLE_EVERGREEN).

Strict No-Go Rule: High-converting transactional landing pages and legal policy pages are barred from automated text generation and require full manual CRO review.
